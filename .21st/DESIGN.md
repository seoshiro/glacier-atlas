# GLACIER

An imagined expedition, told as a cartographic art object. Cold off-white, deep evergreen ink, ice teal and sparse red survey marks. Hero first: expansive terrain, large GLACIER wordmark, intentional small annotations. Then route, field journal and collectible expedition card. Use original assets and system typography. Restrained angular rules, no dashboard panels or app-editor chrome. Fiction is explicit. Mobile prioritizes readable text and tactile controls. Respect reduced motion and keyboard focus.
